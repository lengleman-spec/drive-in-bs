Guidelines

Help us create a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone.

General Guidelines

  • Speed limit: less than 10mph
  • No re-entry or rain checks
  • No photography or video of the movie screen is permitted
  • Play area closes once the movie begins

Parking Guidelines

  • Enter and park at your own risk
  • Two cars between each set of poles. Please park next to a pole allowing space for another vehicle
  • Parking spaces are first come, first serve
  • Lift gates and rear doors cannot exceed the height of your vehicle
  • Use parking lights when entering or exiting

Safety Guidelines

  • Do not leave your car running during the movie
  • Everyone must wear shoes outside the vehicle
  • Children must be accompanied by an adult
  • No alcoholic beverages allowed on property
